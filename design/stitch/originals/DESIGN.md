## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the vigilance, authority, and fluid clarity of an enterprise AI operational co-pilot. Built for founders, operators, and technology executives, it translates complex systemic data into instantaneous comprehension. The personality is protective, hyper-intelligent, calm, and exquisitely tactile.

The design movement is **Liquid Glassmorphism & High-End Material Design**, drawing directly from Apple iOS dark-mode interfaces, Dynamic Island morphing containers, and translucent widget layers. Interfaces consist of deep obsidian glass substrates floating over subtle, dark atmospheric abysses. Luminous radial energy wells (cyan for autonomous intelligence, emerald for infrastructure health, rose for critical intervention) bleed through heavy background blurs, delivering an immersive spatial feel while maintaining ruthless data legibility.

## Colors

The color system operates on an obsidian foundational canvas (`#0A0D14` and `#030712`) enriched with deep navy-indigo ambient underlays. Because this is an enterprise operational guardian, light does not emanate from white flat planes, but rather from optical luminescence within dark translucent matter.

### Functional Palette
- **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: The core cognitive driver. Used for AI agent interactions, primary focus rings, key metric highlights, and live inspection states.
- **Cyber Violet (`#7928CA`)**: The synthetic AI depth accent. Anchors secondary intelligence layers, predictive paths, and autonomous workflow executions.
- **Status Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Complete operational integrity across endpoints, SSL validity, and optimal revenue paths.
- **Status Rose (`#F43F5E`)**: Outages, severe risk alerts, and high-impact rollback triggers.
- **Status Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Degraded performance, SSL expiry advance warnings (90d/30d), and impending bottlenecks.
- **Status Iris (`#6366F1`)**: Maintenance modes, synthetic test runs, and passive auditing.

Specular highlight borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` fading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) define every physical boundary. They simulate the light caught along the chamfered rim of custom curved glass.

## Typography

Typography embodies the technical precision of an aerospace cockpit filtered through Apple’s industrial minimalism. 

- **Primary Typeface (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity with friendly humanist finishes, mirroring the proportions and optical weight of modern device system fonts across display titles, UI navigation, and narrative AI briefings.
- **Monospace & Telemetry Typeface (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Manages the operational telemetry—domain health weight calculations (25% Website, 25% Lead Gen, 15% Performance, etc.), tabular incident logs, SSL expiry countdown timers, and prompt-execution sequences.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- Maintain tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across all analytical cards and health meters to avoid visual jitter during live updates.
- Section tags and analytical status headers utilize `label-caps` in uppercase tracking (`+0.06em`) with 60% opacity foreground color.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a responsive **12-column liquid grid** on desktop, shifting to **6-column on tablet**, and a **single stacked column on mobile**. Content modules exist as modular glass tiles, emulating iOS home screen and dashboard widget architecture.

### Density & Rhythms
- Base unit: `4px` (`0.25rem`). Standard layout components utilize `16px` (`space-md`) or `24px` (`space-lg`) internal breathing room.
- Widgets snap to uniform grid heights (e.g., Small: 160px, Medium: 340px, Large: 520px) to form cohesive masonry dashboards.
- Section headers and analytical groupings are separated by `space-xl` (`32px`) to maintain separation across overlapping blurred layers.
- Form factors leverage fluid container max-widths: Desktop at `1440px`, tablet breakpoints at `768px`, and mobile at `390px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through **physical optical physics (Liquid Glass)** rather than opaque dropshadows. Visual tiers exist along a 4-level z-axis:

1. **Canvas Substrate (Ground 0)**: `#0A0D14` obsidian black tinted with deep radial gradient flares (e.g., 600px blur ambient spheres placed behind critical metrics).
2. **Glass Tile Base (Level 1)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` fill with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(180%)`. Border: 1px continuous solid line with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
3. **Floating Island & Active Controls (Level 2)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` fill with `backdrop-filter: blur(40px) saturate(200%)`. Box shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`, with an inner top specular glow of `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
4. **Modals & Command HUDs (Level 3)**: `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.75)` backdrop blur with an external halo glow keyed to the context's operational status (e.g., cyan glow for AI prompt execution, rose glow for confirmation of critical business rollbacks).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts the iconic **Apple continuous squircle and capsule geometry**. 

- **Cards & Widgets**: Use large radius squircles (`28px` to `36px` on desktop, `24px` on mobile), delivering an organic physical presence reminiscent of iOS hardware and Lock Screen widgets.
- **Interactive Controls & Badges**: Default to pure capsules (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), echoing iOS Dynamic Island elements and pill toggles.
- **Input Fields & Embedded Surfaces**: Use inner nested squircles with a matching concentric inset (`border-radius: max(0px, parent-radius - padding)`).

## Components

### 1. Tactile Glass Buttons
- **Primary / Action Button**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `backdrop-blur-xl`, with a subtle vertical gradient (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` at top to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` at base). Features an ultra-fine 1px specular top highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`).
- **Press State**: Transitions instantaneously with a physical spring curve (`scale(0.96)` and slight brightness depression to simulate depressing frosted physical glass).
- **AI Direct Action**: Infused with an inner radiant glow of Electric Cyan (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25), 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`).

### 2. Status Capsules & Live Activity Badges
- Modeled on the iOS Dynamic Island. Deep black/translucent pill (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`) with an embedded pulsing status bead (6px diameter with a soft 4px aura).
- States:
  - *Operational*: Green bead (`#10B981`) with emerald micro-glow.
  - *Degraded*: Amber bead (`#F59E0B`).
  - *Outage*: Rose bead (`#F43F5E`) with active ripple animation.
  - *Maintenance*: Violet bead (`#6366F1`).

### 3. Glass Dashboard Metric Cards
- Layered widget containers with an inner ambient gradient reflecting the dominant score category health (Website, SEO, Security, etc.).
- Includes a top-right capsule chip for percentage weights and high-contrast tabular typography for numerical readings.
- Outer border utilizes continuous 1px linear stroke gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.2) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.02) 100%)`.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Translucent hollow pill with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` background and `backdrop-blur-md`.
- Leading icon rendered in muted silver (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), transitioning to glowing `#00F0FF` on focus.
- Focus state activates an external 2px cyan ambient beam without harsh solid outlines.

### 5. Multi-Step Incident & Rollback Action Sequence Cards
- Sequenced operational paths (`Problem → Evidence → Recommendation → Risk → Approval → Rollback`).
- Each step is rendered as an interconnected glass bead node with vertical or horizontal glass connector conduits that light up sequentially as automated verifications pass.
