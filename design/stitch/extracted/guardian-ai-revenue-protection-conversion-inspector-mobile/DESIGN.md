## Brand & Style

### Personality & Vision
This design system sets the standard for an autonomous AI Digital Business Operations and Revenue Protection platform. The visual and interactive posture is authoritative, vigilant, hyper-precise, and mission-critical. It operates as an executive command bridge—translating complex digital telemetry (uptime, lead flow, conversion leaks, SEO anomalies, security vulnerabilities) into definitive, grounded business actions.

### Target Audience & Tone
Engineered for founders, operators, agency leaders, and executives across SMBs and enterprises. The interface evokes unshakeable reliability, razor-sharp intelligence, and immediate clarity under duress. It avoids decorative fluff in favor of high-density telemetry, lucid visual hierarchy, and decisive executive summaries.

### Aesthetic Foundation
A high-contrast, modern dark-mode aesthetic rooted in deep space midnight tones, reinforced by crisp micro-borders, refined glassmorphic surfaces, and luminescent status indicators. This is not casual SaaS; it borrows visual cues from aerospace command stations and high-frequency trading terminals while retaining pristine consumer-grade polish.

## Colors

### Color Strategy
The palette prioritizes dark operational legibility. Ambient background darkness allows luminous semantic signals to direct the operator's attention instantly.

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Anchors primary brand identity, decisive CTAs, and active navigational nodes.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Cyan Telemetry):** Designated for AI reasoning pathways, real-time data streams, and active algorithmic diagnostics.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Growth):** Reserved for healthy operational state indicators, verified lead capture, and revenue safety confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F19` - Deep Slate Midnight):** The foundational dark base canvas that maximizes contrast and eliminates backlight fatigue during continuous monitoring.

### Semantic Triage Roles
- **Critical Risk (`#F43F5E` - Rose Alert):** Broken conversion funnels, checkout failures, dropped forms, and security breaches. Emits a localized glow overlay.
- **Elevated Warning (`#F59E0B` - Amber Watch):** Degrading response times, expiring SSL certs, SEO crawl friction, or missed review signals.
- **Operational Normal (`#10B981` - Healthy):** All revenue pathways, monitoring heartbeats, and lead hooks verified active.
- **Executive Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** AI COO insights, automated audit findings, and dynamic optimization triggers.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet sculpted curves provide contemporary authority and executive composure. Used across high-level dashboard summaries, audit titles, and business metric hero headers.
- **Body & Data Labels (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and structurally rigid. Inter delivers flawless optical clarity inside dense data matrices, uptime timelines, logs, and telemetry cards.

### Scaling & Display Guidelines
Display metrics exceeding `48px` (e.g., the 0–100 Digital Health Score) must render in tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) with tightened tracking (`-0.04em`) to maintain strict optical balance against surrounding widgets. Small labels (`label-sm`) serve as micro-status tags and category markers, utilizing uppercase styling and expanded tracking for instant legibility in dark environments.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The layout adheres to a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`min-width: 1280px`) with a maximum container bounding width of `1600px` for high-density command decks.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px (`2rem`) outer canvas margins. Multi-widget telemetry decks (e.g., uptime sparkline + lead flow gauge + incident breakdown) display in 4-column and 8-column blocks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px (`1.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Executive metrics stack into 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins. Telemetry cards fold into a vertically stacked stream ordered strictly by severity: Critical Issues -> Digital Health Score -> Active Workflows.

### Spacing Cadence
Component internal layouts respect an 8pt architectural rhythm:
- `space-xs` (4px): Form element labels to input boundaries, inline status pill gaps.
- `space-sm` (8px): Icon-to-text distances, badge internal padding, list item stacks.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding for standard data widgets, form field vertical gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card internal padding for executive summaries and audit modals.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section layout spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Tiers
Depth is achieved via surface tonal shifts, micro-borders, and radial illumination rather than murky, diffuse black shadows.

1. **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`):** The ground layer. Unlit and non-interactive.
2. **Surface Raised (`#111827`):** Primary monitoring cards and sidebars. Elevated with a crisp hairline border (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
3. **Surface Overlay / Popover (`#1E293B`):** Action drawers, diagnostic tooltips, dropdown menus, and issue resolution modals. Supported by a dual shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
4. **Glassmorphic Deck:** Translucent layers reserved for telemetry overlays and navigation headers. Uses `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `background: rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.75)`.

### Luminous Borders & State Halos
To denote system risk levels without changing card backgrounds:
- **Healthy Edge:** `border: 1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3); box-shadow: 0 0 16px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`.
- **Critical Failure Edge:** `border: 1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.4); box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.25)`.
- **AI COO Active Edge:** `border: 1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35); box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

### Shape Language
The design system employs a refined geometric radius (`roundedness: 2`) that balances high-tech precision with modern SaaS approachability.

- **Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard buttons, text inputs, status chips, table rows, and dropdown panels.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Metric panels, audit summary cards, telemetry viewports, and AI recommendation containers.
- **Modals & Overlays (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px):** Slide-out command drawers, incident inspection dialogs, and setup wizards.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for dynamic status markers (e.g., `MONITORING ACTIVE`, `CRITICAL REVENUE PATH FAILED`) and interactive numeric badge tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Indigo):** Solid `#4F46E5` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, subtle top-edge inner highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Hover initiates a vibrant indigo glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#1E293B` micro-border and `#F9FAFB` text. Hover transitions to `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `border-color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- **Destructive / Incident Action:** Solid `#F43F5E` or dark surface with glowing `#F43F5E` hairline outline.
- **AI Recommendation Trigger:** Gradient border running from `#4F46E5` to `#06B6D4` with an interactive subtle pulse animation.

### Status Badges & Chips
- High-contrast, ultra-compact (`label-sm`, uppercase).
- Structure: 6px pulsating dot on the left, followed by concise status copy.
- Backgrounds utilize a 10% opacity fill corresponding to the semantic state (e.g., `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)` for healthy), bound by a 20% opacity border.

### Telemetry Cards
- Core wrapper uses `Surface Raised` (`#111827`) with `1rem` corner rounding and subtle borders.
- Top partition: Title (`label-lg`), semantic indicator badge, and contextual 3-dot telemetry controls.
- Central zone: Large tabular metric (`headline-lg` or `headline-xl`) paired with 24-hour micro sparklines or uptime bar arrays.
- Bottom partition: Grounded business impact label (e.g., `Est. Revenue Loss: $4,200/hr`) rendered in `text-secondary`.

### Input Fields
- Dark slate fill (`#0B0F19`) set against cards (`#111827`), creating a recessed viewport effect.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Focus State: Border transitions instantly to `#4F46E5` with an external focus ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.25)`.
- Monospace font styling applied specifically to API keys, webhook URLs, and server response logs.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square (`checkbox`: 4px radius) and circular (`radio`: full round).
- Unchecked: `#0B0F19` fill with a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` border.
- Checked: `#4F46E5` fill with a crisp white tick/dot icon, surrounded by an ambient indigo corona.

### AI Insight & Remediation Action Cards
- Differentiated by an inset gradient accent (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.04))`).
- Highlights the definitive chain: **Problem → Evidence → Business Impact → Recommended Action**.
- Features an integrated "Authorize Fix" toggle with explicit safety confirmation states to prevent unintended automated alterations.
